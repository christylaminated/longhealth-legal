This Privacy Policy explains what information LONGHEALTH collects and how it is used. LONGHEALTH is operated by Christy Lam, an independent iOS developer based in the United States.

LONGHEALTH is built to keep your information on your iPhone. The current version of the app does not send your answers, routine, progress or health readings to any server. We do not use third-party analytics, we do not show advertisements, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

## What the app stores on your iPhone

To build and adapt your routine, LONGHEALTH stores the following on your device:

- **Your answers from onboarding and your profile**: first name (optional), gender, age range, goals, activity level, the time you have each day, where you exercise and what equipment you have, wake and bed times, skincare habits, diet preferences and foods to avoid, interest in traditional Chinese practices, and any physical limitations or notes you choose to share.
- **Your body constitution answers**, if you take the optional constitution quiz.
- **Your routine**: the plans generated for you and their history.
- **Your progress**: which practices you complete each day, your weekly check-ins, and readings you note yourself (for example energy, sleep quality or skin comfort).
- **Your notification preferences.**

This information stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer backups) according to your own backup settings, which Apple controls.

## Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, LONGHEALTH asks for **read-only** access to sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, step count and cardio fitness (VO2 max). We use these readings only to show you trends alongside your routine and to adjust your plan.

- LONGHEALTH never writes anything to Apple Health.
- Health readings stay on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
- Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold or shared with third parties.

You can turn off access at any time in iOS **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → LONGHEALTH**.

## Subscriptions and payments

LONGHEALTH offers auto-renewing subscriptions. All purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. The app receives only the information Apple's StoreKit provides about whether your subscription is active.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push notification service or server is involved.

## What we do NOT collect

- Your email address or phone number
- Your location
- Your contacts, photos, calendars or other content on your device
- Advertising identifiers or any tracking data
- Payment details (Apple handles all billing)

## How your information is used

Information stored by the app is used only to:

- Build your personalized routine and choose practices, teas, meals and rituals that fit your goals, schedule, diet and limitations
- Show your progress and trends
- Adapt your routine based on your weekly check-ins

It is not used for advertising, profiling for third parties, or any purpose unrelated to the app's features.

## Optional features in future versions

Future versions may offer optional sign in with Apple to back up your plan, and optional AI-assisted personalization. If we add these, we will update this policy before they are released, explain exactly what is sent and to whom, and ask for your permission in the app before any of your information leaves your device. These features will always be optional.

## Deleting your data

You can delete everything LONGHEALTH has stored at any time: open the **Profile** tab, tap **Privacy & data**, then **Delete all my data**. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.

Deleting your data does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in iOS **Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions**.

## Your privacy rights

Because your information stays on your device and we do not hold a copy of it, you are in control of it directly: you can view and edit your answers in the app, and delete them as described above. If you have a question about your data or want to exercise a right under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, email us and we will help.

## Children

LONGHEALTH is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Data security

Your information is stored in the app's sandboxed storage on your iPhone and protected by iOS data protection, which encrypts it when your device is locked. Keeping a passcode on your iPhone helps keep it secure.

## Health information

LONGHEALTH is a wellness app, not a medical device, and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. See our [Terms of Use](./terms.html) for more.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, let you know in the app.
