## Frequently asked questions

**How is my routine chosen?**
From your answers during setup: your goals, how much time you have, where you exercise, your schedule, diet and any limitations. Every practice in your routine shows why it was chosen for you.

**Can I change my answers?**
Yes. Open the **Profile** tab and edit any section. LONGHEALTH will offer an updated routine, and your current one stays until you accept the new one.

**Why did my routine change?**
After each weekly check-in, LONGHEALTH adjusts your routine: practices that felt too hard get lighter, ones you skipped get swapped, and ones you enjoyed are kept.

**How do I connect Apple Health?**
Open the **Progress** tab and follow the prompt to connect. LONGHEALTH only reads data and never writes to Health. You can change access any time in iOS **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → LONGHEALTH**.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Go to iOS **Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → LONGHEALTH → Cancel Subscription**. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**I already subscribed, but the app is asking me to pay.**
On the subscription screen, tap **Restore purchases** while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

**How do I delete my data?**
Open the **Profile** tab, tap **Privacy & data**, then **Delete all my data**. This can't be undone. Deleting your data doesn't cancel your subscription, so cancel that separately in Settings.

**Is LONGHEALTH medical advice?**
No. LONGHEALTH is a wellness app and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Check with a doctor before starting new exercise or diet routines, and stop anything that causes pain or dizziness.
